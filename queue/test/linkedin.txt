Most students don't miss deadlines because they're lazy. They miss them because the deadline lived on page 7 of a PDF they read once in September.

That's the problem we built GRIND. to fix.

A typical semester means 5 courses, 5 syllabi, and dozens of dated assessments spread across files, course pages and emails. Each one has a weight. A 10% quiz and a 40% final don't deserve the same attention, but in a to-do list they look identical.

So we started with one move: upload a syllabus, and GRIND. pulls out every date and every weight. You review it, fix anything odd, and it's on one screen.

From there it gets practical. What's due in the next 14 days, ranked by weight. Your real weighted average, not a guess. And when it's time to study, Study Studio builds a timed quiz, a mock exam or revision notes from the weeks you pick.

It's free for students, in English or French.

We're early, and we're building this with students, not for them. If you teach, advise or parent a university student: what's the one thing they always find out too late?
