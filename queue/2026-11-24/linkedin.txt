Your final covers 12 weeks. Your slides live in 3 different places.

Some are on the course site. Some came as email attachments. The rest sit in Downloads as lecture7 (2).pdf.

Finals start in a few weeks for a lot of students, and this is the part nobody plans for. Before you can study week 9, you have to find week 9.

Finding one file takes a minute. Finding 12 of them at 10 PM, with a 40% final on Friday, uses up the evening you set aside to study.

We see it as a deadline problem as much as a file problem. The final shows up on your list. But the files you need for it don't.

That's why GRIND. keeps your files inside each course, in the same place as its deadlines and weights from the syllabus. Now the slides sit right next to that 40% final.

It also changes what Study Studio can do. With the files in the course, it can build a practice quiz on weeks 6 to 9 from your own slides, not generic questions.

So when you sit down for the final, you start with week 1. Not with a search bar.
