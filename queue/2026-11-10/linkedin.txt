One course pings you every week. The other stays quiet until a 60% final.

Most weeks, the loud one wins your evenings.

Say you're taking two courses this term. Course A has a 2% quiz every Friday and a lab report every other week and inbox reminders. Course B has a midterm worth 40% and a final worth 60%, and nothing else.

For six weeks, Course B asks for nothing, so it gets nothing. You care about it. There's just nothing on your list telling you it's time.

Then the midterm is 9 days out, and you're opening the week 1 slides for the first time since September.

The Friday quizzes still count. But one 2% quiz and a 40% midterm shouldn't get the same evening.

We kept running into this while building GRIND. A list sorted by date alone rewards whatever course is loudest.

So GRIND. reads the weights from your syllabus and shows what's due in the next 14 days, with the weight next to each item. The 40% midterm appears two weeks out, right beside Friday's 2% quiz, and you can see which one deserves tonight.

Quiet courses still need a plan. They just don't send reminders.

Students: which of your courses is the quiet one this term?
