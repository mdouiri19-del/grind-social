A 58 on a midterm feels like the whole course is gone. That midterm was worth 25%.

So 75% of your grade hasn't happened yet.

But that's not how it feels on the day the mark comes back. You see one low number on the course page and you do the rest of the math in your head, late at night, and it always comes out worse.

Here's the real math. A 58 on something worth 25% gives you 14.5 points out of 25. There are still 75 points left to earn.

If you want to finish at 70, you need 55.5 of those 75. That's about 74% on everything left. Hard, but very doable.

But most of the panic comes from not knowing where you stand. That's the part we wanted to fix.

GRIND. reads the weights from your syllabus and shows your current average based only on what's been graded so far. After one midterm, that's the 25% you've done, not 100%. Next to it, you see what's still coming and what each item is worth.
