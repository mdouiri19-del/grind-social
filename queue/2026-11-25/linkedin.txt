Your final is worth 40%. The best study guide for it is the midterm you'd rather forget.

Most of us check the mark once, close the tab and never look at it again.

But a marked midterm is a list of what you don't know yet, written the way your prof tests. Nothing you write in the last 2 weeks will be that specific.

So before new notes, start a mistake log. 1 line for every wrong answer on every quiz and midterm this term: the question in your own words, and why you missed it.

Then sort the reasons. Most land in 3 piles: didn't know it, misread it, ran out of time. Each pile needs its own fix, and rereading chapter 4 only fixes the first.

Now the habit. Once a week until the final, cover the answers and redo every line. 2 right in a row and it's gone.

The list shrinks. That's the point.

And when the final mixes those topics with new ones, you've already handled the old mistakes.
