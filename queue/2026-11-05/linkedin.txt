Your midterm doesn't cover all 12 weeks. Your practice quiz shouldn't either.

That's the first rule behind Study Studio, the study side of GRIND.

Before it makes anything, it asks you one thing: the weeks you're studying. Then it builds a timed quiz, a mock exam or revision notes from your own course files, for those weeks only.

The easier build would quiz you on all 12 weeks at once. More questions. Looks more complete.

But say your next midterm covers weeks 6 to 9. Four weeks, not 12.

A quiz on all 12 spends a big part of your evening on material that won't be on that paper. Which means the questions you miss might not be the ones that count this week.

So the week picker comes first, even before you choose the format.

The second rule was about where the questions come from. Study Studio works from the files you added for that course, so the quiz sticks to what your class covered.

Now the questions you get wrong point at the weeks you still need. You want to find those 3 days before the exam, not during it.

Students: before a midterm, do you pick the weeks, or study everything?
