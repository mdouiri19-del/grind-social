A 20% midterm and a 5% problem set, due the same week. Which one gets your evening?

Most students give it all to the midterm. Often that's right. Sometimes the small one is where the easy marks are.

Here's the math we use. What an item can still do for your grade is its weight times the points you can still add.

Say 3 extra hours would move your midterm from 70 to 75. That's 5 points on a 20% item, so 1 point on your final grade.

Now say the same 3 hours would take the problem set from 60 to 90, because half of it is questions you already know how to do and just haven't finished. That's 30 points on a 5% item. 1.5 points on your final grade.

The small one wins this time. It doesn't matter more. There's just more left to gain in it.

And it flips once the problem set is done. The right order changes during the week, so redo the math every couple of days.

Before you plan your week, ask two things about each item. What's it worth? How much can I still add?

The date tells you when. Weight and room to gain tell you where your hours go.
