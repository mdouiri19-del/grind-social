GRIND. reads your syllabus. Then it stops and asks you to check.

That pause is on purpose.

Here's how import works. You upload a syllabus PDF. GRIND. pulls out the dates and the weights: the 10% lab report on page 7, the midterm in week 8, the 30% paper at the very end.

Then, before anything lands in your list, you see what it found and check it against the PDF. Only what you confirm goes into your 14-day view.

We could skip that step. Import would feel faster.

But a syllabus is messy. Dates move after week 1. One course writes "Week 9," another writes "Nov 6." And if a student trusts a wrong date, they're the one who pays for it.

So the rule we build by is simple: GRIND. does the digging, you make the call. A quick check beats a missed deadline.
