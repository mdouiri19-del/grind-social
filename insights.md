Top patterns
1. Contrarian "X is a trap" hook (open loop, names a familiar thing and says it betrays you). Reels reach far more than feed posts so far.
2. Seasonal relevance (reading week, midterms) in October.
3. Plain, concrete feature line ("what's due in the next 14 days") works for the sign-up image.

Bottom
- Generic brand line hooks ("Your semester, a little more under control.") as a cover: low reach.
- Instruction hooks posted late evening got little time to collect: judge again next week.

Steering for this week
